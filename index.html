Insights

Process, not people

The board finally has real, self-reported status

Due dates and priority are still blank across the board, so it's not live tracking yet, but it's the richest single "who's doing what" source so far. See the Team section for what it says.

Spend

TikTok is burning cash below breakeven

September final: ROAS 0.93 vs. Meta's 1.20, on 1.3× Meta's spend, for a worse cost per sale (425 vs. 301 SAR). Last week it was 0.87 vs. 1.16, and the first days of October read 0.41 (small sample). Still the clearest lever to improve CAC without adding budget.

Data quality

Two churn numbers, same dashboard

Overview's rolling 30-day churn rose to 27.2% (from 23.9% on Sep 27). Retention's recommended "Proper SaaS" card only shows the current month, now Oct 1–3 (3.1%), so September's figure on that method isn't readable as a number. Neither should anchor a KR target until Maher confirms which one is canonical.

Quick win

A named, buyable fix for involuntary churn

9.9% of people with failed payments are on cards that "don't support purchase" (mostly Saudi cards on international rails). Retries only recover 15.0% of these, and they cause 21.9% of first-charge failures for brand-new customers. Enabling Mada is a bounded, concrete fix sitting directly in the data.

Revenue ceiling

Members leave before the upsell even starts

3.28-month average lifetime against a 12-month program, 27% completion on average. Half of starters are gone by their 3rd payment. Almost all Mind Power / Ezdhar revenue is currently unrealized.

Pipeline health

All data feeds are healthy again

Settings → Sync Status still shows every source healthy as of Oct 3 (GHL Contacts, Stripe, Meta, TikTok, GHL chat and orders). GHL Funnels, stale since ~Aug 25, is no longer listed. It isn't clear yet whether it was fixed, replaced by the new "GHL orders" feed, or retired.

Worth watching, not concluding

Week-1 completion may not predict 30-day retention

97% vs. 94% retention regardless of week-1 completion (n=178 vs. 205), on a still-small sample. If this holds, churn may not be the onboarding-activation story it's assumed to be.
